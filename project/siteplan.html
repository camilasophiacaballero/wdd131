Website Planning Document

Descubre Honduras

Site Name

Descubre Honduras

The name was chosen because it represents a website dedicated to showcasing the beauty, culture, and tourist attractions of Honduras. The site will help visitors discover destinations and plan their trips.

Optional domain: descubrehonduras.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about the most popular tourist destinations in Honduras. Visitors can learn about attractions, explore travel recommendations, view destination details, and contact the site for additional information.

Scenarios

  • What are the best tourist destinations to visit in Honduras?
  • What activities can I do in Tegucigalpa and nearby areas?
  • Which destinations are recommended for families?

Color Schema

  • #0077B6 (Blue) - Headers, navigation, and footer.
  • #90E0EF (Light Blue) - Page background and content sections.
  • #FFB703 (Gold) - Buttons, links, and accents.

Typography

  • Poppins - Headings and navigation menu.
  • Arial - Body text and paragraphs.
